Apaga

Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · Last updated 18 November 2026

The short version

  • Apaga has no user accounts and no servers of its own. Everything you enter (quit dates, cravings, slips, journal, reasons and goals) stays on your phone.
  • The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and some technical data to show and measure those ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first.
  • Your quit and health information is never sent to us or to advertisers.
  • Apaga Plus removes all ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

1Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how House of Mmminq ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Apaga mobile application for Android ("Apaga" or "the App"). For the purposes of data protection law, House of Mmminq is the controller of the limited processing described here.

Contact: houseofmmminq@gmail.com

2Information that stays on your device

Apaga is designed so that the personal content you create stays on your phone. This includes:

  • The products you track (for example cigarettes, vapes, nicotine pouches or cannabis), your quit or "keep for now" choices, "stopped since" dates, usage and price figures
  • Cravings, triggers, slips, journal entries, mood notes and insights
  • Your reasons for quitting, if-then plans, savings goals, badges and progress
  • Your settings, such as reminders, the daily thought, your preferred audio voice and other preferences

This information is stored locally in the App's private storage. It is not uploaded to us, and we cannot see, access or recover it. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Device backup: if Android backup is turned on, Android may include the App's data in your device backup in your Google account. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings and Google's Privacy Policy, not by us. You can turn device backup off in Android settings.

3Advertising (free version)

The free version of Apaga is funded by banner ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). Ads never appear on the Craving SOS screen, during exercises, in the Calm tab or in the audio player.

To show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the App may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android advertising identifier)Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and, with your consent, personalised ads
IP address and the approximate location derived from itShowing ads relevant to your region, and fraud prevention
Device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device, and diagnostics
Ad interactions (ad impressions and taps)Measurement, reporting and fraud prevention
Diagnostic and performance data from the ads SDKStability of ad delivery

This data is collected and processed by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We receive only aggregated reports (such as the number of ad impressions), which do not identify you.

We have asked Google not to show ads from sensitive categories such as alcohol and gambling. None of the quit, health or journal information you enter in Apaga is shared with Google or used to choose ads.

4Your ad choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the App, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform (an IAB TCF-certified consent tool) asks whether Google and its ad partners may use your data for personalised advertising. If you decline, you may still see limited or non-personalised ads.
  • Changing your mind: you can review or withdraw your consent at any time in Apaga under Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads on some devices).
  • No ads at all: Apaga Plus removes all ads. With Plus, the App makes no ad requests and doesn't show the consent message.

5Purchases (Apaga Plus)

Apaga Plus is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment information under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. The App receives only the information it needs to unlock Plus (whether the purchase was made, and a purchase token). We never receive your name, card details or billing address.

6Notifications, audio and permissions

  • Notifications: reminders and encouragement are scheduled and generated on your device. You can turn them off in the App or in Android settings.
  • Audio playback: the self-hypnosis tracks are bundled with the App and play offline. The App uses a media playback service so you can control playback from the notification and lock screen.
  • Internet access: used to load ads and their consent message, and to verify purchases with Google Play.
  • Vibration: optional, for breathing exercises.

Apaga doesn't access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

7Health information

Apaga provides support, tracking and relaxation. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Any health-related information you enter, such as cravings, slips, sleep or mood notes, or nicotine replacement use, stays on your device and is not sent to us or to third parties.

8Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): personalised advertising, and storing or accessing information on your device for advertising where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): showing non-personalised ads to fund the free version, ad measurement, and fraud and abuse prevention.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): providing Apaga Plus after you buy it.

9Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays there until you delete it in the App, clear the App's data in Android settings, or uninstall the App. Because we hold no copy, deletion on your device is final. Data processed by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Because we don't store personal data on our own systems, most of these rights are exercised directly in the App (by deleting your data or using Ad privacy options) or with Google. You are always welcome to contact us at houseofmmminq@gmail.com.

If you are in the EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live.

11International transfers

Google may process advertising data in countries outside the European Economic Area, including the United States. Google relies on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

12Age requirement

Apaga is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

13Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. Communication between the App and Google's ad and billing services is encrypted (HTTPS). Keep your phone protected with a screen lock, because anyone who can unlock it can open the App.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when we add features or services. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will let you know in the App.

15Contact
